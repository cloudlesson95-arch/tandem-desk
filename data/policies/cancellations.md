# Cancellations

## Which rentals can be cancelled

Only booked rentals that have not started can be cancelled. An active rental cannot be cancelled; the customer can return the bike early instead, and the early return rules in the refund policy apply.

## Cancellation fees

- More than 48 hours before the start: free cancellation with a full refund.
- Within 48 hours of the start: a fee of 50% of the first day's rate.
- No-show without cancelling: the first day is charged in full and the rest is refunded.

Plus members can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the start.

## Severe weather

When an official severe weather warning covers the rental start, cancellation is free at any time before the start. See the weather policy.

## Confirmation

The support assistant asks the customer to approve every cancellation before it is made.
