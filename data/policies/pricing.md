# Pricing

Velomarsh Bike Rental is a fictional bike-rental service. All prices are in US dollars and exclude 8% sales tax, which is added at checkout.

## Daily rates

| Bike | Price per day |
|---|---|
| City bike | $18 |
| Hybrid bike | $24 |
| Road bike | $28 |
| City e-bike | $32 |
| Mountain bike | $35 |
| Cargo bike | $40 |
| Tandem bike | $45 |
| Kids bike | $12 |

A rental day is 24 hours from the pickup time. Every rental includes a lock, a helmet and a front and rear light set at no extra cost.

## Hourly rentals

City bikes can be rented for $6 per hour and city e-bikes for $10 per hour, with a minimum of 2 hours. An hourly rental of 4 hours or more is charged as one full day. Other bike types are rented by the day only.

## Multi-day discounts

Rentals of 7 days or more get 15% off the daily rate. Rentals of 14 days or more get 25% off. Discounts do not stack with each other, but they do stack with the Plus membership discount.
