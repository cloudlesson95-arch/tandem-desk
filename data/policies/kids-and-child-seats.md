# Kids bikes, child seats and trailers

## Kids bikes

Kids bikes cost $12 per day and fit children aged 5 to 12. Child helmets are free.

## Child seats

Rear child seats fit children from 9 months to 4 years old who weigh up to 22 kg. A child seat costs $5 per day and can be fitted to city and hybrid bikes only.

## Trailers

A child trailer carries up to two children with a combined weight of up to 45 kg and costs $12 per day. Trailers can be towed by city, hybrid and city e-bikes.

## Booking

Child seats and trailers should be added to a booking at least 24 hours in advance, because stock is limited.
