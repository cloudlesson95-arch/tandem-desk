# Late returns

## Grace period

Every rental has a 30-minute grace period after the scheduled end time. Returns within the grace period are not charged.

## Late fees

After the grace period, the late fee is $10 for each started hour. Late fees for any 24-hour period are capped at the bike's daily rate.

## Overdue and unreturned bikes

A bike that is more than 24 hours late without contact from the customer is marked overdue, and support tries to reach the customer. A bike that is more than 48 hours late without contact is reported as unreturned, and the full security deposit is kept.

## Avoiding late fees

Extending the rental before its scheduled end time avoids late fees entirely. See the extension policy.
