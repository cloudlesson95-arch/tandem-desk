# Pickup and return

## Desks and opening hours

Velomarsh has three desks:

- Harbour Desk: 8:00 to 20:00 every day
- Central Station Desk: 8:00 to 20:00 every day
- Riverside Park Desk: 9:00 to 18:00 every day

## Returning to another desk

Bikes can be returned to any desk at no extra cost. One-way returns between desks are free.

## After-hours drop box

Central Station Desk has an after-hours drop box for bikes, open at all times. A drop-box return counts as returned at the time the bike is dropped, as recorded by the drop box camera. The bike is inspected the next morning, and the deposit release starts then.
