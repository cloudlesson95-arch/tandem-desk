# Age and identification

## Minimum ages

- Riders must be at least 16 to rent a bike on their own.
- Riders must be at least 18 to rent an e-bike.
- Riders under 16 can ride when a parent or guardian rents the bike and signs the rental agreement.
- Kids bikes are sized for ages 5 to 12.

## Identification

The person picking up the bike must show a government-issued photo ID. The name on the ID must match the name on the booking. A booking can be transferred to another adult at the desk if both people are present.
