# Theft and loss

## Locking

Whenever a bike is left unattended, it must be locked to a fixed object with the lock provided.

## Reporting a theft

A stolen bike must be reported to Velomarsh within 24 hours, and the customer must file a police report within 48 hours.

## Liability

When the bike was locked correctly and both reports were made in time, the customer's liability is limited to $150 for standard bikes and $400 for e-bikes, cargo bikes and tandems. Otherwise the customer is liable for the bike's full replacement value. The damage waiver does not cover theft.

## Lost accessories

A lost lock key costs $30 and a lost lock costs $40.
