# Security deposits

## Deposit amounts

A security deposit is held on the customer's card at pickup:

- $100 for city, hybrid, road, mountain and kids bikes
- $250 for city e-bikes and cargo bikes
- $300 for tandem bikes

The deposit is a hold, not a charge.

## Release of the deposit

After the bike is returned and inspected, the hold is released within 3 business days for credit cards. Holds on debit cards can take up to 10 business days to disappear, depending on the customer's bank.

## When the deposit is used

The deposit covers unpaid late fees, damage charges, cleaning fees and lost accessories. Any amount taken from the deposit is itemised in an email sent on the day of return inspection.
