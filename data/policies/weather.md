# Weather

## Severe weather

Severe weather means an official storm warning or heat warning issued for the rental area. When severe weather covers the start of a rental, the customer can cancel for free or reschedule at any time before the start, even within 48 hours.

## Rain

Rain alone is not severe weather. Normal cancellation fees apply for rain, and rain covers are available at all desks for free.

## During a rental

When a severe weather warning starts during an active rental, the customer can return the bike early and get a full refund of the unused days.
