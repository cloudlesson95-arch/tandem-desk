# Payment methods

## Accepted payment

Velomarsh accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards and debit cards. Apple Pay and Google Pay can be used in the app. Desks do not accept cash.

## When customers are charged

The full rental price is charged at booking. Extension days are charged when the extension is approved. Late fees and damage charges are taken from the deposit or charged to the card on file.

## Card safety

Support staff and the support assistant never ask for a full card number in chat or by phone. Card details are entered only in the app's secure payment form.
