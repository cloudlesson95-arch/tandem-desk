# Damage and cleaning

## Inspection at pickup

Customers should inspect the bike at pickup and report any existing damage within 15 minutes, at the desk or with photos in the app. Damage reported in that window is never charged to the customer.

## What is charged

Normal wear, such as tyre wear, minor scratches and brake pad wear, is never charged. Other damage is charged at repair cost from the price list:

- Bent wheel: $60
- Broken derailleur: $85
- Damaged e-bike display: $120
- Damaged e-bike battery: up to $450
- Frame damage: the bike's replacement value

## Damage waiver

The optional damage waiver costs $4 per day and caps the customer's damage liability at $50 per rental. It does not cover theft or deliberate damage. Plus members get the damage waiver free.

## Cleaning fee

A $20 cleaning fee applies only when a bike is returned covered in mud or sand that needs a wash. Ordinary road dirt is free.

## Disputes

A damage charge can be disputed within 14 days of the return by contacting support.
