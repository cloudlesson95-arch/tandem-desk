# Helmets

## Free with every rental

Every rental includes a free helmet. Adult sizes XS to XL and child sizes are available at all desks.

## When a helmet is required

Riders under 18 must wear a helmet. All e-bike riders must wear a helmet, whatever their age. Helmets are strongly recommended for everyone else.

## Hygiene

Helmets are cleaned and disinfected after every rental, and disposable liners are available on request.

## Lost or damaged helmets

A helmet that is not returned is charged at $25. A helmet that was in a crash must be returned and replaced at no charge, because it can no longer protect a rider.
