# Tandem bikes and group bookings

## Tandem bikes

A tandem bike costs $45 per day and needs a $300 deposit. The front rider (captain) must be at least 16. The rear rider (stoker) can be 10 or older when the captain is their parent or guardian.

## Group bookings

Groups renting 6 or more bikes for the same dates get 10% off the daily rates. Group bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance by one lead booker, who is responsible for all bikes in the group.

## Mixing discounts

The group discount does not combine with the multi-day discount; the larger of the two applies.
