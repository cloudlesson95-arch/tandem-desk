# Rental extensions

## Who can extend

Booked and active rentals can be extended before their scheduled end date, through the support assistant, the app or any desk. Returned and cancelled rentals cannot be extended.

## How long

Each extension adds 1 to 3 days to the rental's end date. A stay that needs more than 3 extra days at once must be booked as a new rental.

## Price

Extension days are charged at the bike's normal daily rate, and the Plus membership discount applies. The extra days are charged to the card on file when the extension is approved.

## Confirmation

An extension is only confirmed after the customer approves it. The support assistant always shows the new end date and asks for approval before changing a rental. Extending before the scheduled end avoids late fees.
