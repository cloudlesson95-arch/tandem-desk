# Plus membership

## Price

Plus membership costs $49 per year and renews automatically. It can be cancelled at any time, but membership fees are not refunded.

## Benefits

- 10% off all daily rates, including extension days
- The damage waiver for free on every rental
- Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the start, instead of 48 hours
- Priority service on the roadside assistance hotline

## Combining with other discounts

The Plus discount stacks with the multi-day discount. It does not stack with the group discount.
