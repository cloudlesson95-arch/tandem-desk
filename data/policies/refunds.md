# Refunds

## Timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of approval.

## Early returns

When a customer returns a bike early, each unused full day is refunded at 50% of the daily rate. Partial days are not refunded. Hourly rentals are not refunded for early returns.

## Mechanical failure

If a bike breaks down through no fault of the rider, the customer gets a free replacement bike or a full refund of the remaining rental days, whichever they prefer.

## Disputes

Customers can dispute a charge within 14 days of the rental end by contacting support. Disputes are answered within 5 business days.
