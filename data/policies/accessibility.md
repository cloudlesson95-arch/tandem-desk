# Accessibility and adaptive bikes

## Adaptive bikes

Velomarsh offers hand cycles, adult tricycles, recumbent trikes and side-by-side tandems for riders with disabilities. Adaptive bikes should be reserved at least 72 hours in advance so a fitted bike is ready.

## Price

Adaptive bikes are charged at the city bike rate of $18 per day, whatever their type. A companion riding on a side-by-side tandem rides free.

## Help at the desk

Staff help with fitting, seat adjustments and a short practice ride at no cost. All desks have step-free access, and assistance animals are welcome.

## Other needs

Customers can ask the support assistant or a desk about other access needs. The team tries to accommodate every request but cannot guarantee equipment that is not in the fleet.
