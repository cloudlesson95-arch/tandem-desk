# Riding rules

## On the road

Riders must follow local traffic laws, ride on roads and bike paths rather than sidewalks, and use the provided lights between dusk and dawn.

## Loads

Cargo bikes carry up to 80 kg in the front box. Other bikes may carry up to 10 kg in the rear rack. Passengers are allowed only on tandems and in approved child seats or trailers.

## Prohibited uses

Riding under the influence of alcohol or drugs, racing, stunts, and modifying the bike are not allowed. City and hybrid bikes must not be used on unpaved mountain trails; mountain bikes are allowed on marked trails.

## Breaking the rules

Damage caused while breaking these rules is not covered by the damage waiver.
