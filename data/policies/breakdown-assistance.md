# Breakdown assistance

## Roadside hotline

The roadside assistance hotline is open from 7:00 to 21:00 every day. Plus members are served first.

## Replacement bikes

Within 15 km of a desk, Velomarsh brings a replacement bike within 60 minutes. The replacement is free when the breakdown is caused by a mechanical fault. When the rider caused the damage, the damage policy applies.

## Flat tyres

Every bike carries a puncture repair kit and a pump, and customers may fix a flat themselves. Flat tyres are never charged.

## Outside the service area

More than 15 km from a desk, the customer brings the bike to the nearest desk, and Velomarsh refunds a taxi or rideshare fare up to $30 when the breakdown was a mechanical fault.
