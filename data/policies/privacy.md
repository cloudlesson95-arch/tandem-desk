# Privacy and the support assistant

## Personal data in chat

The support assistant removes email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, bank account numbers (IBANs) and street addresses from messages before any AI model sees them. Customers never need to share these details in chat.

## What the assistant remembers

With each conversation, the assistant may save a few short notes, such as preferred bike types or desks, and a summary of the conversation, so later conversations can pick up where they left off. These notes never contain contact or payment details.

## Viewing and deleting memories

Customers can see every saved note in the memory panel and delete any of them at any time. Deleted notes are removed immediately.

## Account deletion

A request to delete an account and all its data is completed within 30 days.
