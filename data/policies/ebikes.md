# E-bikes

## Performance

City e-bikes have pedal assist up to 25 km/h and a range of 60 to 80 km on a full battery, depending on terrain and assist level. Batteries are fully charged at pickup.

## Charging and battery swaps

A charger is included with e-bike rentals of 2 days or more. Customers can swap a low battery for a charged one at any desk for free. Returning a bike with an empty battery is not charged.

## Rules

E-bike riders must be at least 18 and must wear a helmet. City e-bikes are for paved roads and bike paths only; they must not be ridden on unpaved trails.
